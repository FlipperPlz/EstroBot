# Privacy Policy for EstroBot

EstroBot is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide reminders and calculate HRT statistics, EstroBot stores the following information:
- **Discord User ID**: Used to associate your profile with your Discord account.
- **Administration Details**: Method (Oral, Injection, Transdermal), Ester, Dosage, and Schedule.
- **Timezone**: Used to send reminders at the correct local time.
- **Reminders**: Any custom daily reminders you set within the bot.

## 2. How We Store Information
- All data is stored locally on the server where EstroBot is hosted.
- Information is stored in JSON format, indexed by your Discord User ID.

## 3. Data Sharing
- **No information is shared with third parties.**
- Your data is used exclusively by EstroBot to provide you with reminders and statistics.
- Data is never sold, rented, or distributed.

## 4. Your Control
- You can view your stored data at any time using the `/stats` command.
- You can modify your data using the `/setup` command (Edit Profile).

## 5. Security
While we store data locally and do not share it, please be aware that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage users not to share sensitive personal information beyond what is required for the bot's functionality.

## 6. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the bot administrator.
